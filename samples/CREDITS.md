# Sample credits

Each folder holds one recording every minor third from A0 to C8 (30 MP3s).
Files are unmodified copies of the sources below.

## piano/

Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm, licensed under
[CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
MP3 subset as distributed by Tone.js at https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/.
Original recording: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3

## tanpura/

A seamless 13.8-second loop cut from "Tanpura 10" by sankalp
(https://freesound.org/people/sankalp/sounds/153262/), released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). A big tanpura
tuned Pa – Sa – Sa – low Sa with Sa on C#3, recorded at Tapan music center. The
loop is taken from 62.6 s, normalised, with its end crossfaded into its start,
and stored as lossless FLAC.

## usul/

A seamless 11.6-second bendir loop (four cycles of an 8-beat usul) played and
recorded by Ozan for Pantonal, licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Cut from his
recording at 3.1 s, with a 30 ms crossfade at the seam, stored as lossless FLAC.

## Every other folder

From the FluidR3_GM soundfont by Frank Wen, rendered to MP3 by
[midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts) and licensed
under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
Source: https://gleitz.github.io/midi-js-soundfonts/FluidR3_GM/

`choir/` is the soundfont's "Choir Aahs". The other folders keep the
soundfont's instrument names: accordion, acoustic_guitar_nylon, celesta, cello,
church_organ, clarinet, flute, french_horn, kalimba, marimba, music_box, oboe,
orchestral_harp, pan_flute, pizzicato_strings, steel_drums, string_ensemble_1,
trumpet, vibraphone, violin, xylophone.
