Play it like a theremin

The theremin, invented by Leon Theremin around 1920, is played without touching it.

  • Right hand: up is higher, down is lower.
  • Close your hand to stop the sound.
  • Left hand (optional): up is louder.

pantonal

Pantonal turns drawings into music. Draw lines on a canvas and hear them played: left to right is time, bottom to top is pitch. Free Thinker lets lines glide through every pitch; Chromatic Thinker places them on the twelve notes of the chromatic scale, with instrument layers, scores and export to MuseScore; Air Thinker turns the camera into a theremin you play with your hands.

Inspiration

Pantonal was inspired by an interactive drawing-to-music installation at the House of Music Hungary (Magyar Zene Háza) in Budapest, discovered during a visit in October 2026. It stood in the exhibition’s section on twelve-note music, hence the name. Pantonal is an independent project, not affiliated with the House of Music Hungary.
